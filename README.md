# Learn 2 Speak (L2S)

An AI language tutor that actually talks back.

Most language apps teach you words. L2S gets you speaking. Take a quick lesson, then practice real conversations with an AI tutor and cartoon characters who have their own voices, then get honest feedback on how you did.



## What you can do

**Learn**
- Fresh 5-question lessons generated by AI every time (vocabulary, grammar, translation)
- XP and daily streaks to keep you coming back
- 9 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Italian, Portuguese, Korean
- Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels

**Talk to your AI tutor**
- Type or use your microphone
- The tutor replies out loud and gently corrects your mistakes in your native language
- It knows your name, goal and interests, so conversations feel personal

**Practice real situations**

| Situation | Who you talk to |
|---|---|
| Job interview | A professional interviewer |
| Public speaking | A speaking coach and audience |
| Debate | An opponent who challenges your arguments |
| Talk to your crush | Maya, a friendly person you'd like to impress |
| Restaurant | A waiter |
| Airport / travel | A check-in agent |
| Doctor visit | A doctor |
| Small talk | A friendly stranger |

Each character has their own look and their own voice. When you finish, you get a score for fluency, grammar, vocabulary and confidence, plus what you did well, what to improve, and better ways to say your own sentences.

**Make it yours**
- A short setup on first launch: your name, goal, what you do, and your language
- Design your main tutor, Podi: boy or girl, skin, hair, outfit, colours, headphones, glasses and background

## Built with

- HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, all in a single `index.html` (no build step, no dependencies)
- [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (free tier) for lessons, chat and grading
- Web Speech API for voice input and spoken replies
- Hand-drawn SVG characters, generated in code
- Hosted on Netlify

## Getting started

1. Open the live site, or download `index.html` and open it in your browser.
2. Create a free API key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
3. Follow the setup: tell the app about yourself, paste your key, and customise Podi.
4. Start learning.

**Tip:** use Chrome or Edge. Microphone input needs one of them, and the site must be served over https (Netlify does this for you).

## Deploy your own

1. Fork or clone this repo.
2. Create a new site on [Netlify](https://app.netlify.com/) and connect the repo, or drag `index.html` into Netlify Drop.
3. No build command and no environment variables are needed.

## Privacy

L2S has no backend and no database. Your API key, name and settings are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). Your messages are sent straight from your browser to Google's Gemini API, and nowhere else.

## Good to know

- Free-tier Gemini keys have rate limits. If you hit them, wait a moment and try again.
- Voices come from your device and browser, so how they sound depends on what's installed. Installing extra text-to-speech voices for your language gives each character a more distinct sound.
- If Google retires the default model, change the model name in Settings. No code changes needed.
